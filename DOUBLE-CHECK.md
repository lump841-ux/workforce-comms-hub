# Twanova Double Check

A repeatable product-review process for every Twanova application before public release.

1. **Mission:** Define the customer, core problem and measurable result. For Workforce Communications Hub, connect temp worker, staffing agency and client supervisor around assignments, status, replacement coverage and accountability.
2. **Market:** Research direct and adjacent competitors. Compare features, pricing tiers, onboarding charges and contract terms. Record dated evidence and customer feedback.
3. **Economics:** Model infrastructure, database, messaging, translation, AI, payment processing, support and taxes for small and large customers. Check margins under normal and heavy use.
4. **Interface:** Inventory every page, image, link, button, form, search field and upload. Test actual outcomes, broken links, empty/error states, and responsive layouts.
5. **Roles:** Test complete workflows with separate worker, agency manager and client accounts. Confirm updates, permissions, notifications, escalations and records.
6. **Devices:** Test iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, desktop Chrome, Safari and Edge, plus tablet. Capture screenshots and repeatable results.
7. **Trust:** Verify account access controls, tenant separation, durable data, backups/restoration, privacy, secure uploads and session protection.
8. **Billing:** Test all tiers, contract terms, signing, trial conversion, payment failure and account activation in a sandbox before using real payments.
9. **Reliability:** Load-test expected usage, check logs and monitoring, rehearse deployment rollback and database recovery.
10. **Launch gate:** Publish a dated Double Check report stating PASS, FAIL or NOT TESTED for each requirement, with issues, evidence and a clear go/no-go decision. Unresolved critical failures block launch.

A successful website deployment does not by itself mean the app passed Double Check. Apply this framework to each new Twanova app and major release.
