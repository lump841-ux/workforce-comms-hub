Private test use only. Use made-up names and sample information. Do not enter real payroll, IDs, or confidential client information. Existing accounts and passwords are unchanged.

Set up your test

  1. Client: In the agency dashboard, add a sample client such as “TEST — Warehouse A.” Create a supervisor invitation and copy its link.
  2. Workers: Invite two sample workers from the Workers area. Use distinct test email addresses. Copy each invitation link; invitees choose their own passwords. Copying a link does not send an email or SMS.
  3. Separate sessions: Keep the agency on your main browser. Open the worker invitation on another device or browser, and the client invitation in a third session. Two ordinary tabs in one browser share a login; they are not separate accounts.
  4. Assignment: Create a shift for the first worker and the sample client. For an attendance test, use a shift that starts shortly after you are ready. Confirm the assignment appears on the worker and client dashboards.

Prove the connection

  1. Worker selects On My Way, then Running Late with a sample ETA. Refresh the agency and client dashboards and compare the saved information.
  2. Worker selects Arrived. Use the full worker dashboard for clock-in, messages, and other controls not available in the mobile beta.
  3. Send an in-app message and reply from the intended recipient. Check the other account receives it.
  4. Create a separate test shift to test replacement coverage. Keep the second worker available for that date; the current replacement pool checks availability, not the new proposed skills matching.
  5. Log out and back in using the same credentials. Confirm the test data remains. Record any error, the account role, device, and action that caused it.

The mobile beta has connected shift actions, but some photo and messaging screens are unfinished. This test does not certify public-launch readiness or 500-worker capacity. Do not use the separate Practice Mode to validate shared data.
